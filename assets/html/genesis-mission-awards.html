Key findings

  • Broad, not concentrated: the largest science field holds only ~14%; ten fields each hold at least 5%.
  • Physical-science core (Materials, HEP/Nuclear, Quantum, Fusion) makes up roughly 38% of the portfolio.
  • Critical Minerals & Rare Earths stands out as a notably specific policy priority.
  • Labs punch above their headcount: ~15 DOE labs took ~31% of awards (avg ~6 each) vs. ~120 universities (avg ~1 each).
  • Performer type tracks the science: universities dominate discovery domains (HEP/Nuclear, Biology, Quantum); labs lead mission-operational domains (Earth/Climate, Nuclear Fuel-Cycle, Subsurface, Fusion). Industry clusters in Materials/Manufacturing and Fusion — the commercialization-adjacent work.
